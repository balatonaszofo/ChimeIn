## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production logo mark for a playful personal Chrome extension named Chime In, which helps Reddit readers stop lurking and join conversations. Design a single simple mascot icon: a rounded lime-green speech bubble with two dark forest-green dot eyes, a warm small smile, and a speech-bubble tail suggesting one little foot stepping forward. A tiny friendly raised nub on one side can suggest a wave, but keep the mark extremely simple. Flat solid shapes, bold clean silhouette, balanced geometry with a touch of handmade warmth. Main lime #c8ec91 and dark forest #263321, matching the product. One centered mark, square composition, generous small safe margin. It must read clearly at 16, 32 and 128 pixels, with no fine details. Transparent background, no enclosing app tile, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no typography, no text, no watermark. This is an original friendly speech bubble mascot, not the Reddit mascot or logo.
